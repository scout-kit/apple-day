Signups arrive from a form

Parents fill in whatever form the group already uses. The import reads it, matches people against the roster, and shows you what it could not place before anything is saved — a name spelled two ways, a child who has moved section, a row with no times ticked at all.

Availability can also be typed in directly, because not everybody uses the form. A paper signup at a meeting, a phone call, a parent changing their mind on the night — all of it goes in the same place, and somebody who never filled the form in can still be given hours and put on the schedule.

Shops are recorded once, not once a year

There is a library of real places, shared by every year: the address, the map link, who to ask for at the shop, and the hours it is actually open on each day of the week. A shop open until ten at night is recorded that way even if you only ever staff until eight.

That is what lets the board tell you a shop is shut at an hour you have staffed — which is how a youth ends up standing at a locked door on a Saturday morning.

Each year then picks which of those places it is using, in what order. Setting up a new year means copying last year's list and switching off whatever closed.

"Closed" and "nobody has said" are kept apart. Only the first is a reason to stop somebody scheduling — refusing on the second would make a shop whose hours were never recorded impossible to staff at all.

Siblings stay together

Two children from one family can be paired, and the schedule keeps them at the same shop for the same hours. Pairing is a relationship rather than a note on one of them, so it means the same thing said from either end and cannot be half-set.

Changing the shape of a year

An event is a named thing — "Apple Day, October 4–5" or "Spring bottle drive" — rather than a year. It chooses which days it runs, the hours staffed on each, how long a shift is, and how much each shift overlaps the one before it. An overlap is a handover: the next pair arrive while the current ones are still there, collect their supplies and walk out together.

Some events do not have shifts at all, and can say so. Every day then becomes one slot and people come for the duration.

Changing the hours after people are scheduled does not delete anybody's shifts. Shifts that no longer line up with an hour are flagged on the board so you can move them.
